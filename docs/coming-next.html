Coming next

Features still to be built. Nothing here has a date; what comes first depends on who is using OsaBooks and what they need. To ask for one, or for something not listed, open an issue on GitHub.

New Zealand

  • ACC work levy by classification unit, for sole traders, from ACC's yearly rate schedule.
  • Provisional tax by the ratio option, taking out a fixed asset sale before the ratio is applied.
  • A Kiwibank payment file. BNZ, ANZ, Westpac and ASB files are written already.

Other countries

  • United States: income tax beyond a single filer with the standard deduction (other filing statuses, the QBI deduction, credits and state income tax); Form 940 and W-2; payroll for more states (Illinois, Ohio, Georgia, North Carolina and on, by size).
  • Filed returns to compare with, as New Zealand books do with myIR's GST returns: Australia's BAS, South Korea's VAT return and US sales tax returns.

Moving from other software

  • QuickBooks: invoices, bills and customers brought in as documents, with due dates and aged receivables and payables, rather than as journals.
  • MYOB: open invoices and bills, customers and suppliers, and items.
  • Sage 50, Wave and Zoho Books.

Money in other currencies

  • Wise balances in other currencies, converted at each day's rate.
  • Foreign currency: open receivables and payables revalued at the balance date, credit notes across currencies, and quick entry in another currency.
  • Currencies with three decimal places, such as dinars and rials, held to the third decimal.

Books kept online

  • The same choices for every set of books: open, rename, make a copy, clear and delete, for books online and books in a folder alike, with when each was last opened.
  • SimpleFIN bank feeds for books kept online (today on the desktop app only).
  • Bank feeds, AI and the morning run on your own Supabase project.
  • Payment reminders from your own email address.
  • Card payments with Stripe that show as soon as they are paid, and for books kept in a folder.
  • Lock dates enforced by the server, so a change in a locked period is refused, not only noted.

AI and connectors

  • More for your AI app: the trial balance, GST returns and journals.
  • One connector for all your books, rather than one for each set.

Everyday use

  • Undoing a merge of two accounts in full, with everything it moved.
  • US business meals, half deductible.
